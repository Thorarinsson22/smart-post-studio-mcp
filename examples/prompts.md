# A week of prompts (copy-paste)

Written for Smart Post Studio's MCP server; most wording carries to any scheduler with MCP, the guardrails won't.

**Monday — lay of the land (read-only)**
> "What's scheduled across all my platforms for the next seven days? Group it by day, flag any day with nothing on it, and tell me which platforms each post goes to."

**Reach check (run once, and after connecting anything new)**
> "Which platforms can I post to right now, and which of them publish from the cloud versus from my desktop app?"

**Draft, don't schedule**
> "Draft posts about [the thing] for Instagram, Threads and LinkedIn. Same idea, different openings: Instagram leads with the visual, Threads reads like a reply to a friend, LinkedIn leads with the lesson. Keep Threads under 500 characters. Save them all as drafts — don't schedule anything."

**Schedule one you've read**
> "Schedule the Instagram draft for Tuesday at 10:00 my time with this image: https://example.com/image.jpg"

**Fill the gaps**
> "For each empty day you flagged, propose one post recycled from my last month of published posts — new angle, not a repeat. Drafts only."

**Move / cancel**
> "Move Thursday's LinkedIn post to Friday 09:00." · "Cancel the X post about the webinar."

**Friday — did it go out?**
> "For everything scheduled this week, show me the per-platform result and list anything that failed with the reason."

Three we'd never run: anything that asks the AI to post "now" (the tool doesn't exist), bulk-scheduling 30 days without reading the drafts, or giving the schedule permission on day one.
